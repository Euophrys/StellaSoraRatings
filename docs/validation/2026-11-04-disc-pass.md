# Disc pass: every disc specced (2026-11-04)

Goal: now that there's a team for every element, model every disc that still had a scripted part with no spec ("scripted, not modelled") or a trigger the data couldn't name ("uptime 1 x N assumed"). There were 48 such discs. `python -m ssr.inspect scripted-discs` now lists none, and no disc modifier is assumed.

## User answers

- **Dodges:** one every 10 s (`dodge_interval`, `dodge:main`). Only Zesty Curiosity uses it.
- **Getting hit:** roughly every 10 s in practice, but the boss doesn't attack while broken, and it depends on the boss and on melee vs ranged. We use every 20 s (`hit_taken_interval`, `hit_taken:main`), and none while broken.
- **Shields last their full duration:** most hits are chip damage, and losing a shield is usually a mistake.
- **Summer Sanctuary "while the Ultimate is active":** until the ult stops dealing damage, or until the player regains control. Modelled as the main's ult `lock` (`while:in_action:0:ult`).
- **Fly, O Caged Bird:** the user will test how Starlit Hope stacks build. GUESS for now: one per second a Ventus Versatile support is on the battlefield.

## From data

- New Star Prelude's shield: `Effect` 4009020 `Trigger` 19 / condition 29 / param 20 is a 20 s timer, so the shield is up 15 s of every 20 s from battle start (`timer:20`).
- Prayer Beneath Light's shield: `Trigger` 3 (when hit), `CD` 20 s.
- The 3★ "restore 15 Energy at the start of battle" melodies are EffectType 34 and are read as starting Energy (15 of a 237–285 ult cost).

## Engine additions

`timer:<s>`, `dodge:main`, `hit_taken:main` and `on_field_1s:<who>` events; `while:vul:<EET>` (an elemental DMG Taken debuff is up = "VUL") and `while:in_action:<member>:<action>` conditions; `mark: {tags: [...]}` on kits (Noya's Mark is tagged `cyclone`, Nanoha's `blossom`); battle-start Energy (`START_ENERGY`). Shields (EffectType 42) are noted as "shield (no damage)" and tracked as statuses in the specs. Tests: `tests/test_disc_events.py`.

## What changed in existing specs

- "When the main is hit" buffs (Soda Shopping, The Shining, Unknown Fragrance, Cage of Roses, ★Rolling Life★, Delusion) changed from a 10% GUESS uptime to the `hit_taken:main` event.
- One Shot, One Down's ATK ramp is simulated stack by stack instead of the 120 s average.
- "vs VUL" lines (One Shot, One Down, Sparkling Moment, The Meow, Lemon & Black Tea, Colors Pierce the Grey) now use `while:vul`. They used to be dropped.
- Defensive "reduces DMG taken" lines are dropped explicitly, so they no longer appear as assumed.
- Several data parts had a second sentence the data never encoded, which was silently missing. These are now specced: Deer's Song harmony 1 (Skill DMG after a skill cast), Chronicles of Spring harmony 1 (Auto Crit DMG per Drifting Snow), Summer Sanctuary melody (Lux DMG on a Lux Mark during the ult), Wine Warm, Lamps Alight melody and harmony 1, Dawn After Winter harmony 1, Little Paradise harmony 1, and the Flowing Fragrance / Spring Blossom debuffs.

## Follow-up 2: dodges need control; dodge on cooldown only with the potential (same day)

User: you can't dodge in skill or ult animations, but you're immune then, so you don't need to. The 1 per 10 s already
accounts for that. Chitose's Tremble and Fall gives the big last auto strike right after a dodge (it was modelled only
after Torrent Flash). In the Nazuna-main team, Gerie does most of the damage, so Nazuna can dodge all the time like
Mistique.

- Dodges due in a lock wait for it to end. The average rate is kept, with dodges at least 3 s apart.
- Tremble and Fall: after each dodge, the last strike (both hits) gives Edge. User-timed: dodge pressed 3,18 -> hit 4,13 and 4,19 -> 5,14 (+0.833 s); 14,00 -> 14,25 + smaller hit 14,28 (+0.833 / +0.933 s). Modelled as a follow-up action `dodge_strike` cast when the 10-frame dodge ends; autos resume after it from strike 1 (GUESS).
- `dodge_spam` replaces Mistique's `dodge_interval: 3`. It applies only when the build has the listed potentials.

## Follow-up 4: Chitose's dodge loop; Starlit Dash doesn't stack (same day)

User timings (30 fps): after the last strike's second hit, her normal string's first hit comes 0.767 s later (3,16 ->
4,09; 7,00 -> 7,23). Dodging right after that second hit (16,28 -> dodge 16,29) gives the next big hit at 17,24
(+0.833 after the dodge). The Mirror Image (Mirror Blade) follows the dodge strikes too.

With Tremble and Fall, her autos are now a **dodge loop**: dodge, last strike +0.833 / +0.933 s, next dodge at
+0.967 s. That's 246% of ATK per 0.967 s vs 694% per 5.033 s for the full string. The string is also cut short by every
Torrent Flash, so it rarely reaches its last strike. Serpent's Glide adds one Serpent per dodge strike, and Mirror
Blade mirrors both hits at 60%. The loop is a potential replacing the `auto` action (`add_actions: {auto: ...}`); its
`dodge_cancel` makes a dodge at each loop's start, and the periodic dodges stop. Waltz of Ripples alone keeps
`dodge_spam` (3 s).
